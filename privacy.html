Fatebound Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026
Developer: midblade4295
App: Fatebound (package com.fatebound.game)

This page is the privacy policy for the Fatebound mobile game published by midblade4295 on Google Play.

Who we are

Fatebound is made and published by midblade4295. Questions about this policy: midblade4295@gmail.com.

What the app does

Fatebound is a single-player dice arena game. Progress is stored on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no player profile on our servers.

Information we collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photos, contacts, or payment card details.

Game progress (gold, relics, map state, settings, and similar play data) is saved locally on your device using the app’s on-device storage. That data stays on the device unless you back the device up yourself.

Internet use

The app may use the internet to load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). Google may receive a standard network request from your device when those fonts load. We do not send your game save to Google.

The Android package includes an internet permission so the WebView can load those resources. We do not run an ad network or analytics SDK in the current build.

How we use information

On-device save data is used only to keep your game progress and settings. We do not sell data. We do not use the game to build advertising profiles.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share game saves with third parties. Google Play may process install and purchase records under Google’s own policies when you download the app from Play.

Children

Fatebound is a general-audience fantasy game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account.

Retention and deletion

Because there is no Fatebound account and no server-side player database, there is no account for us to delete.

To remove local game data, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall Fatebound. That deletes the on-device save.

Security

Saves live on your device. Protect the device with the usual lock screen. We do not operate a cloud save service in this version.

Your choices

You can stop using the app and uninstall it at any time. You can write to midblade4295@gmail.com with privacy questions. If a later version adds accounts or cloud saves, this policy will be updated first.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
